📍 Beverly Hills & Los Angeles
🎂 THE Day!
🌹
Birthday Brunch at The Polo Lounge ↗ · Beverly Hills Hotel
✓ Booked
The legendary pink-and-green Polo Lounge at the Beverly Hills Hotel — as iconic as Los Angeles gets. View brunch menu →
🛍️
Rodeo Drive & Wilshire Blvd Walk
Walk up La Cienega to Wilshire then cut to Rodeo Drive — iconic window shopping. Continue down to Beverly Drive and Canon Drive for charming boutiques and cafés. Very walkable: flat, manicured, beautiful.
🎨
Melrose Avenue Walk
Short drive from SLS to the stretch between La Cienega and Fairfax. Independent boutiques, street murals (the famous Paul Smith pink wall!), and great cafés. Eclectic, artsy — a completely different energy from Rodeo.
🚗
Beverly Hills Drive & Greystone Mansion
Scenic drive through the hills. Stop at the gorgeous Greystone Mansion & Gardens (free entry). Options: Mulholland Drive for the best views, Benedict Canyon for "old LA" mansion vibes.
🥂
Birthday Cocktails at Gemma · Waldorf Astoria Rooftop ↗ 5:30pm
✓ Booked · 5:30pm
Stunning rooftop bar atop the Waldorf Astoria Beverly Hills. Sunset cocktails on the birthday — it doesn't get better.
🍽️
Birthday Dinner at Il Cielo · Beverly Hills ↗ 8pm
✓ Booked · 8:00pm
The grand finale — a beautiful Italian restaurant with a magical garden setting. The perfect birthday dinner.

Wednesday · Walking Tour, Wharf & Dancing
📍 Santa Barbara
🏛️
Santa Barbara Walking Tour · State Street 10am
✓ Booked
Sullivan's 2-hour tour starts at the south entrance of Paseo Nuevo Mall, 715 State St. Covers the Courthouse, Casa de la Guerra, the historic core, and the earthquake rebuild story. Climb the clock tower for panoramic views of the city and ocean. Walkable from the Airbnb (~15 min on foot).
☕
Brunch at Boathouse ↗ · Hendry's Beach
No booking needed
Brunch right on Hendry's Beach — fresh seafood and great ocean views.
🚢
Stearns Wharf Stroll
Walk down from the Funk Zone to the wharf for golden hour. Just 8 minutes on foot from the Airbnb. Watch the pelicans, browse shops, take family photos with the harbor views stretching out behind you.
💃
Free Line Dancing Lesson! ↗ 6pm – 8pm
Downtown Kickin' Country at 716 State St. Free and open to everyone — one of those only-in-SB experiences. Might be the highlight of the whole trip!
